01 · Color

Flat or nothing

Every color is a flat fill. Ink is the only color allowed on borders and shadows. Yellow + purple is the brand pair — purple (two tones) is the main secondary accent. Text is ink on light fills, paper on dark fills (ink, teal, signal, grey). Never yellow text on paper.

04 · Neo-brutal tokens

Border, shadow, space

The whole style is three moves: a thick ink border, a hard offset shadow, and flat fill. Press feedback = the element moves into its shadow (120ms, ease-out). Nothing fades, nothing glows.

Border SM
2px · badges, small controls
Border MD
3px · default everything
Border LG
4px · heroes, page rules
Shadow SM
3px 3px 0 · buttons
Shadow MD
5px 5px 0 · cards
Shadow LG
8px 8px 0 · features
  • Radius: square (0) or full pill (999px). Never anything in between.
  • Spacing: 4px scale — 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64.
  • Shadows never blur and are always down-right — ink on light fills, brand yellow on ink fills (so border and shadow never blend black-on-black).
  • Shadows belong to raised, clickable surfaces only — buttons, cards, timers, charts. Form fields (inputs, selects, textareas, sliders, checks, radios, toggles) stay flat; focus is an ink outline.
  • Motion: press = translate by the shadow offset, shadow collapses to 0. 120ms ease-out. That's the only transition the brand owns.
